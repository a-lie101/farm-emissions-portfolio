## OFCAF program, federal
- $704.1M total fund, part of Agricultural Climate Solutions. 2025-2028 expansion added $300M to 13 recipient organizations. Program ends March 2028. Source: AAFC program page. [solid]
- Three funded categories: nitrogen management, cover cropping, rotational grazing. [solid]
- $100,000 maximum per farm business across ALL OFCAF delivery organizations Canada-wide, April 2022 to March 2028. Sources: OSCIA program guides, SAW program terms. [solid]
- Funded GHG reductions cannot be resold into offset credit programs (clause in OSCIA and PWCP terms). Not shown in v1 UI. Have this ready if someone asks about stacking carbon credits on top of OFCAF. [solid]

## Ontario delivery (OSCIA, relevant to Libro)
- OSCIA delivers OFCAF in Ontario. Ecocert also delivers some OFCAF in Ontario. Source: OSCIA. [solid]
- Cover cropping: 65% cost share (75% for applicants new to OFCAF), max $30,000 per project. Planting equipment 50%, max $30,000. Multi-year projects up to 2-3 seasons. Source: OSCIA program guide. [solid]
- Nitrogen management: $30,000 equipment cap across all approved applications 2022-2028. Knowledge Sharing Event required. New acres required. Source: OSCIA program guide. [solid]
- Feb 24, 2026 nitrogen intake opened 9:00 AM ET, fully allocated by ~10:05 AM ET, first come first served. Currently between intakes, next not announced. Source: secondary grant-tracking site quoting OSCIA. [verify: confirm on ontariosoilcrop.org the day before the call]

## Saskatchewan delivery (SAW, relevant to Gavelin)
- Saskatchewan Association of Watersheds delivers OFCAF in SK via SWEAP, a $40M program, 2025-2028 phase. Categories: nitrogen management (polymer coated urea, inhibitors, legume rotations, equipment), cover cropping, rotational grazing. Source: program summaries. [verify amounts on saskwatersheds.ca]
- Requirements: new practice or new acres post Feb 2022, licensed agrologist endorsement, one award per parcel, landlord and tenant cannot both apply on the same land. Rebate style with invoices and before/after photos. Sources: SAW and PWCP fact sheets. [solid]

## Alberta (context, demand signal)
- RDAR delivers. 2026-2027 intake: 900+ applications in 30 days, roughly double prior years. Intake paused May 15, 2026, reopened with $10,000 project minimum. Sources: RDAR, Alberta Farmer Express. [solid]

## Nitrogen economics
- Saskatchewan Ministry of Agriculture 2026 Crop Planning Guide uses ~$830/tonne urea. At 46% N: $1.80/kg N. Source: SaskToday reporting the guide. [solid for the guide figure]
- 2026 global urea prices were exceptionally volatile: spiked to ~US$690-900/t during Middle East supply disruption, corrected to ~US$400/t by mid-year on Chinese exports. Config band $1.40-2.10/kg N reflects this. Sources: Trading Economics, Argus via Manitoba Co-operator. [verify: refresh week of call]
- Precision and sensor-based N management literature: 5-45% N savings with little yield effect. Caveats: about a quarter of studies showed economic losses, and variable rate sometimes redistributes rather than reduces total N (Canola Council). This is why the default is 10%, the range is 5-15%, and copy says "may" and "estimated." Sources: ScienceDirect review of sensor-based N application, Canola Council 4R pages. - Gavelin Farms: 768 ha, ~654 t CO2e total, N2O ~77% fertilizer-driven, brown soil zone, land partly grazed. Source: our Holos runs.
